# BLAME SHIFT
Game Off 2026 entry (theme: SHIFT). Night shift at QUIK-E-MART: things keep going wrong,
and it's always your fault -- so shift the blame onto customers. Score-chaser, made in Godot 4.3+ (GDScript), no external assets.

## Play
1. Open Godot 4.3+, Import `project.godot`, press F5.
2. WASD/Arrows move, SHIFT sprints, hold SPACE next to a `!` to frame the nearest customer, P pauses.

## Rules
- Each incident has a timer. If it runs out, the blame lands on you = pink slip. 3 slips and you're fired.
- Victims score by innocence: Teen x1, Mime x2, Grandma x3, Priest x4, Cat x6.
- Cops are witnesses: plant evidence inside a cop's red zone and you're caught.
- Chain frames within 7s for a combo (max x8). Faster plants score more.
- Surviving until 6 AM gives a shift-change bonus, removes one slip, and the next night is harder.
